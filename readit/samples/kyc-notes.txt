KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER BASICS

Financial institutions in Ghana must verify the identity of every customer. Dr. Mensah notes that 2.5 million accounts were reviewed in 2024. The penalty for a breach can reach GH₵ 1,250.50 per day.

Customers are rated low, medium or high risk. High-risk customers need enhanced due diligence, e.g. source-of-funds checks, before the account opens on 01/03/2026.
