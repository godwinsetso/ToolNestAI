Know Your Customer Basics
Financial institutions in Ghana must verify the identity of every customer.
Doctor Mensah notes that two point five million accounts were reviewed in twenty twenty-four.
The penalty for a breach can reach one thousand, two hundred and fifty cedis and fifty pesewas per day.
Risk rating
Customers are rated low, medium or high risk.
High-risk customers need enhanced due diligence, for example source-of-funds checks, before the account opens on the first of March twenty twenty-six.
